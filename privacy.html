Privacy Policy

The "One Brick Short" uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @OneBrick9x to upload that channel's own videos.

  • It requests access to the owner's YouTube account only to upload videos and captions to that same channel.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about anyone else.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sent anywhere except to Google's APIs.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
